03 · Research

A research question instead of buzzwords.

Current question
How can expert knowledge become computable without losing context, provenance and meaning?

Clinical guidelines are the current research setting: a difficult, high-stakes domain where trustworthy knowledge representation matters.

01 · ExtractionClinical entities, recommendations and contextual signals.
02 · AlignmentNormalizing concepts across heterogeneous sources and terminologies.
03 · RepresentationStructures that preserve relationships, evidence and provenance.
